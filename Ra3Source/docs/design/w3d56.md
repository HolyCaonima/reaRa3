## Behavior the oracle locks down

Camera begins clamp the millisecond argument to at least 1, divide by the frame-millis global, and keep a quotient of at least 1. Zoom, height, and angle store the current scalar as the blend start and call `configureEase`. A one-millisecond begin also runs the matching update. Updates increment the step, blend with `subss` / `mulss` / `addss` while the step is still inside the frame count, and snap to the target once the step reaches the frame count. Pitch multiplies both the blend and the snap by `0x3C8EFA35`. Sample translation adds `position - anchor` onto samples from index 2 through the current index; the ledger size of `0056C2B0` ends before the real `ret 4`, and the oracle executes through that epilogue.

`view56SphereVisible` walks planes at `+0x308` with stride 16. The accumulator is `plane.y * cy + plane.z * cz + plane.x * cx - distance`, and `comiss` / `ja` rejects the sphere. `record56WriteFloatUnlessEqual` skips the store when `ucomiss` reports ordered equality, including `+0` against `-0`, and writes otherwise. Extent fill uses `-0 - border` for the first component and `+0 - border` for the second. Bounds intersection resets on a strict miss (`jae`) and, on a hit, takes the max of the mins and the min of the maxes.
